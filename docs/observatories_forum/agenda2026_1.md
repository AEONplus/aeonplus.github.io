# Agenda

The workshop will feature a mix of talks, demonstrations and hands-on hack sessions. 
Topics covered are wide ranging and include observatory software and operations, 
instrumentation and policy with a focus on enabling time domain science.  
Emphasis is placed on what researchers need to accomplish their scientific goals, 
and talks feature the latest scientific drivers and associated technologies in time domain astrophysics.
