# In Case of Emergencies...

We hope you will enjoy your stay in Santa Barbara, but just in case, here is some important safety information.

## Emergency Services
Dial 9-1-1 for Ambulance, Fire Service, Police and Coast Guard. 

## Emergency Room and Urgent Care
For life-threatening emergencies, please dial 9-1-1.  
Emergency Room care is available at:

* Santa Barbara Cottage Hospital, Castillo Street, Santa Barbara Tel: (805) 682 7111  Open 24hrs
* Goleta Valley Cottage Hospital, Patterson Avenue, Goleta Tel: (805) 967 3411  Open 24hrs

For non life-threatening conditions that require prompt care, the following urgent care clinics are available:

* MedCenter Inc, 2954 State Street, Santa Barbara, Tel: (805) 682 7411, Open 10am to 6pm or later daily
* Cottage Urgent Care, 3885 State Street, Santa Barbara, Tel: (805) 569 7351, Open 8am - 8pm daily
* Sutter Urgent Care, 215 Pesetas Lane, Santa Barbara, Tel: (805) 563 6110, Open 8am to 7pm or later daily
* Cottage Urgent Care, 5652 Calle Real, Goleta, Tel: (805) 879 4239, Open 8am to 8pm daily
* Cottage Urgent Care, 7070 Hollister Avenue, #103, Goleta, Tel: (805) 324 9270, Open 8am to 9pm daily

## Emergency Preparedness
While you're visiting Santa Barbara we recommend that you subscribe to the [ReadySBC.org service](https://www.readysbc.org/). 
This website provides early warning alerts and recovery information in the case of extreme weather and natural 
disasters.
