# Food and Drink

## During the day
Refreshments will be provided for morning and afternoon coffee, and lunch will be catered at the museum.  

## Dining Options

For the evening, Santa Barbara has a wide range of excellent restaurants, clustered downtown but also in Goleta.  
A few local favourites are listed below.

### Santa Barbara
#### Casual
No reservations needed, with plenty of seating for groups
* Third Window - The best brewery in town and the best smashburgers anywhere.  They have other food too.
* SB Biergarten - Informal -- German and American casual food.  No reservations, but you don't need them - big, long tables that can accommodate a crowd.
* Eureka! - Craft beer and burgers
* Santa Barbara Public Market - A bunch of food vendors -- good to walk around and find what you want and bring it back to a table.
* Los Agaves - good mexican

#### Mid-scale
Reservations advisable
* Brophy Bros. Santa Barbara - great location for small groups, with views over the marina
* Harry's - Old school red leather booths and old photos of Santa Barbara history with stiff drinks
* Sama Sama - Southeast Asian
* Corazon Comedor - Excellent Mexican
* Zaytoon - Middle Eastern, outdoors in a beautiful setting
* Olio Pizzeria - more casual cousin to the more upscale Italian restaurant next door
* Ca' Dario Santa Barbara - Italian
* Boathouse at Hendry's Beach - unbeatable location right on the beach; very good fish menu

#### Upscale
Reservations highly recommended
* Dom's Taverna - Basque!  Also has a late night menu.
* Arigato Sushi - Best sushi in Santa Barbara
* The Lark - Outstanding local California chef-created fare; pricy.  Reservation required.
* Barbareno - Local upscale chef-created food
* Flor de Maiz - Excellent Mexican near the water
* Loquita - Upscale Spanish Tapas

### Goleta
#### Casual
Reservations generally not needed, with plenty of seating for groups
* Sushi Teri Goleta - Good sushi, can seat small-medium-sized groups
* Los Arroyos Goleta - Mexican, plenty of seating
* Masala Spice Indian Cuisine - Indian with good vegetarian options
* CAYA Restaurant - Californian cuisine
* Pepe's Mexican Restaurant 
* Finney's Crafthouse - American favorites and craft brewhouse

#### Mid-scale
Reservations advisable
* Ca'Dario Goleta - Quiet Italian place ideal for small groups; call ahead for a larger group
* The Ellwood at Goleta Beach - beautiful location on the beach, can accommodate larger groups
